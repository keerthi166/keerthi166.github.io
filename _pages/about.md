Hi, welcome to my personal webpage.

I am a Research Scientist at the IBM Thomas J. Watson Research Center, where I work at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Natural Language Understanding. My research is motivated by a simple but increasingly important question: How can we build AI systems that remain reliable, interpretable, and trustworthy as they learn, adapt, and act in dynamic real-world environments? As large language models (LLMs) and AI agents evolve from passive assistants into autonomous systems capable of planning, reasoning, and taking actions on behalf of people, the central challenge is no longer simply building larger models, but designing systems whose behavior can be understood, controlled, and governed throughout their lifecycle. My research seeks to address this challenge by combining advances in machine learning, reasoning, and human-centered AI to develop intelligent systems that operate safely and responsibly under uncertainty.

I received my Ph.D. from the [School of Computer Science](https://www.cs.cmu.edu) at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu). My doctoral work explored multitask, online, and lifelong learning, focusing on how machine learning systems can continuously acquire and transfer knowledge as tasks and environments evolve. Although the AI landscape has changed dramatically with the emergence of foundation models, these ideas continue to shape my research. I view foundation models not as static endpoints, but as continuously evolving systems that must adapt to changing objectives, new information, and human feedback while maintaining predictable and trustworthy behavior.

My current research includes [trustworthy foundational models](https://research.ibm.com/topics/trustworthy-ai) with an emphasis on [mitigating risks](https://www.ibm.com/granite/docs/models/guardian/) in Large Language Models (LLMs), [Neuro-symbolic AI](https://ibm.github.io/neuro-symbolic-ai/) based on [Thinking Fast and Slow](https://sites.google.com/view/sofai/home), and [reasoning](https://research.ibm.com/topics/knowledge-and-reasoning)/[planning](https://sites.google.com/view/llmsxplanning-lab/home) in LLMs to advance their reliability and interpretability. My publications reflect a sustained effort to bridge learning theory, reasoning, and governance in AI systems, with contributions spanning top-tier venues such as ICML, NeurIPS, ICLR, ACL, EMNLP, AAAI, IJCAI, etc.

My research has resulted in more than 50 publications in leading AI conferences, including NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ACL, EMNLP, AAAI, IJCAI, KDD, and other premier venues. Several of these research contributions have been integrated into IBM products such as [IBM Granite](https://www.ibm.com/granite) and [watsonx](https://www.ibm.com/watsonx), enabling research ideas to influence real-world deployments at scale. I actively work with researchers across academia and industry and serve as a principal investigator or co-PI on several university partnerships focused on trustworthy AI, foundation models, reasoning, and agentic systems. These collaborations allow me to pursue long-term scientific questions while ensuring that the resulting technologies address practical challenges faced by organizations deploying AI today.

I am always happy to collaborate with researchers and students working on related problems. I also actively mentor [students](/students/) on topics spanning trustworthy AI, reasoning, and agentic systems. Feel free to reach out if you'd like to discuss research or collaborations.
